Common Questions

Are your programs really free?

Our open-source tools and resources are 100% free! Our educational programs and curriculum implementation services are tailored to each organization's needs. Contact us to discuss how we can support your cybersecurity education goals.

How can my school use your K-12 curriculum?

Contact us using the form above with "K-12 Curriculum Inquiry" as your reason. We'll work with your school to implement our standards-aligned curriculum and provide implementation support tailored to your needs.

Do you offer services for nonprofits and small businesses?

Yes! We provide high-quality cybersecurity services to nonprofits, schools, small businesses, and underserved communities on a sliding scale. Everyone's budget is different and so is everyone's needs—we'll meet you where you are. Contact us to discuss how we can support your organization.

Do you accept corporate sponsorships?

Absolutely! Select "Donation / Sponsorship" as your contact reason and we'll discuss partnership opportunities. All donations are tax-deductible.

How can I volunteer?

We welcome volunteers! Select "Volunteer Opportunity" and tell us about your background and how you'd like to contribute.
