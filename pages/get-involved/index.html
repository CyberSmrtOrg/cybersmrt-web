Why Your Support Matters

CyberSmrt is a service-disabled veteran-owned 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to bridging the cybersecurity skills gap and protecting underserved communities. We provide free K-12 education programs, sliding-scale security services for nonprofits, and hands-on training for the next generation of cybersecurity professionals. Your involvement makes this possible.
