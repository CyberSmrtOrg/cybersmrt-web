Why Your Donation Matters

CyberSmrt is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit bringing cybersecurity education and affordable managed security services to communities that traditional providers overlook. Every dollar you contribute directly fuels our mission to close the cybersecurity skills gap and protect vulnerable organizations.

100% Mission-Driven

As a service-disabled veteran-owned nonprofit, we operate with lean overhead and maximum impact. Your donations fund curriculum development, free student resources, sliding-scale MSSP services, and career pathways for underserved communities. We reinvest every dollar back into the mission.
