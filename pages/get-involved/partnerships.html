Partnership Options

Why Partner With Us?

CyberSmrt's mission-driven approach creates unique partnership opportunities for organizations that want to make a measurable impact in cybersecurity education and community protection.

Our Partnership Model

We believe in mutually beneficial relationships that advance cybersecurity education, provide real-world learning opportunities, and strengthen community security. Our partners gain brand alignment with a mission-driven nonprofit, access to emerging talent, and opportunities to give back to underserved communities.

Partnership Opportunities

We work with diverse organizations to amplify our impact. Find the partnership model that fits your goals.

🏢

Corporate Sponsors

Align your brand with our mission while supporting cybersecurity education and workforce development. Corporate sponsorships fund program expansion, student scholarships, and community initiatives.

Benefits include:

  • Brand recognition on website and materials
  • Employee volunteer opportunities
  • Access to job-ready talent pipeline
  • Tax deduction for charitable contributions
  • Annual impact reports and recognition
💻

Technology Vendors

Technology companies can provide in-kind donations of software, training platforms, or cybersecurity tools. We integrate partner technologies into our curriculum and MSSP operations.

Benefits include:

  • Product exposure to students and nonprofits
  • Early talent recruitment opportunities
  • Community goodwill and brand building
  • Co-branded educational content
  • Partnership recognition and case studies
🏫

Schools & Districts

Educational institutions can adopt our K-12 cybersecurity curriculum at no cost or on a sliding scale. We provide teacher training, materials, and ongoing support.

Benefits include:

  • Free or low-cost curriculum licensing
  • Professional development for educators
  • Standards-aligned content (CISA, NICE)
  • Career pathway development for students
  • Community and industry connections
🤝

Nonprofit Organizations

Nonprofits can access our MSSP-lite services on a sliding scale and collaborate on community cybersecurity education initiatives.

Benefits include:

  • Affordable managed security services
  • Joint community outreach programs
  • Resource sharing and collaboration
  • Donor and beneficiary data protection
  • Mission-aligned partnership
🎓

Higher Education

Colleges and universities can collaborate on curriculum development, student internships, and research initiatives in cybersecurity education.

Benefits include:

  • Student internship and research opportunities
  • Faculty collaboration on curriculum
  • K-12 to higher ed pathway development
  • Community engagement programs
  • Joint grant proposals and funding
🏛️

Government & Foundations

Government agencies and foundations can fund program expansion, research initiatives, and community impact through grants and contracts.

Benefits include:

  • Measurable community impact metrics
  • Alignment with workforce development goals
  • Evidence-based program evaluation
  • Equity-focused service delivery
  • Comprehensive reporting and accountability
