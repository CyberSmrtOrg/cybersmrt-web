How the Program Works

Our programs blend self-paced online learning with hands-on labs, live mentorship sessions, and real-world projects. Learn at your own pace while building a portfolio that demonstrates your skills to employers.

Phase 1: Foundations (8-12 weeks)

Build core technical skills through guided labs and online coursework. Cover networking fundamentals, Linux/Windows administration, and introductory security concepts.

  • Self-paced modules with hands-on virtual labs
  • Weekly live Q&A sessions with instructors
  • Peer study groups and Discord community

Phase 2: Specialization (12-16 weeks)

Deep dive into your chosen track with advanced labs, capture-the-flag competitions, and mentorship from practicing security professionals.

Prepare for job interviews, build your professional brand, and connect with hiring partners. We support you until you land your first role.
