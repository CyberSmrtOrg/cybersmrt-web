Our Mission

Digital safety shouldn't be a privilege. We provide free cybersecurity education and digital literacy training to communities traditionally excluded from technology resources—delivered in accessible formats, multiple languages, and culturally relevant contexts.

The Digital Divide Is a Security Issue

Underserved communities face disproportionate cybersecurity risks. Seniors lose $3 billion annually to online scams. Rural communities lack access to security training. Non-English speakers navigate technology without language support. Youth inherit digital habits without guidance. We're changing that.

Our Community Programs

Each program is tailored to the specific needs, challenges, and cultural contexts of the communities we serve. All workshops are 100% free and designed for accessibility.

Senior Digital Safety

Empowering older adults to navigate the digital world safely. We cover scam prevention, online banking security, social media privacy, and device protection through patient, judgment-free instruction.

  • Recognizing phishing and romance scams
  • Protecting financial accounts and personal info
  • Safe social media and video chat practices
  • Managing passwords and two-factor authentication
  • Spotting fake websites and tech support scams
  • Privacy settings for smartphones and tablets

Delivery: In-person workshops at senior centers, libraries, and community centers. Large print materials. Patient, hands-on instruction. Family members welcome.

How We Deliver Programs

We meet communities where they are—literally and figuratively. Our approach prioritizes accessibility, cultural relevance, and dignity.

Free and Accessible

All programs are 100% free with no hidden costs. We provide materials, handouts, and follow-up resources at no charge to participants or partner organizations.

  • No registration fees or material costs
