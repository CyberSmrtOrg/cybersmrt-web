🛡️ QR Code Safety Best Practices

  • Always inspect the URL before visiting a QR code link
  • Be wary of QR codes in unsolicited emails or texts
  • Check for misspellings in domain names (common phishing tactic)
  • Avoid QR codes that ask for personal information immediately
  • Use this tool to verify QR codes before scanning with your phone
  • When in doubt, contact the organization directly through official channels
  • Never scan QR codes from unknown sources in public areas
  • Seniors: Be extra cautious of QR codes claiming urgent account issues
