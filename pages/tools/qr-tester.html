🛡️ QR Code Safety Best Practices

  • Always inspect the URL before visiting a QR code link
  • Be wary of QR codes in unsolicited emails or texts
  • Check for misspellings in domain names (common phishing tactic)
  • Avoid QR codes that ask for personal information immediately
  • Use this tool to verify QR codes before scanning with your phone
  • When in doubt, contact the organization directly through official channels
  • Never scan QR codes from unknown sources in public areas
  • Seniors: Be extra cautious of QR codes claiming urgent account issues

Camera Permission Required

Before we request camera access, let's understand what this means for your privacy and security.

🎯 What We Need Access To:

We're requesting permission to use your device's camera to scan QR codes in real-time.

  • Camera only - We don't need or request microphone access
  • Temporary access - Only while actively scanning
  • Local processing - QR codes are decoded on your device, not sent to our servers
  • No recording - We don't record, store, or transmit camera footage

⚠️ Why Browser Permissions Matter:

Browser permissions are your first line of defense against malicious websites. When a site requests camera access, it could:

🚨 Potential Risks if Granted to Malicious Sites:

  • Spy on you - Record video/photos without your knowledge
  • Steal sensitive info - Capture documents, credit cards, or private moments
  • Track your location - Use background objects to identify where you are
  • Profile you - Build behavioral patterns from camera access timestamps

✅ Best Practices:

  • Only grant to trusted sites - Check the URL carefully before approving
  • Revoke when done - Don't leave permissions active indefinitely
  • Watch for suspicious requests - Question why a site needs camera/mic access
  • Use temporary permissions - Some browsers let you grant one-time access

🔒 How to Revoke Camera Permission After Use:

We strongly recommend revoking camera access when you're done scanning. Select your platform:

🌐 Chrome / Edge / Brave:

  1. Click the 🔒 padlock icon or ⓘ site information icon in the address bar
  2. Click "Permissions" or find "Camera" in the dropdown
  3. Change camera permission to "Block" or "Ask (default)"
  4. Or go to: chrome://settings/content/camera

🦊 Firefox:

  1. Click the 🔒 padlock icon in the address bar
  2. Click "Connection secure" → "More information"
  3. Go to "Permissions" tab
  4. Find "Use the Camera" and click "Remove Permission"

🧭 Safari:

  1. Go to Safari → Settings → Websites → Camera
  2. Find cybersmrt.org in the list
  3. Change to "Deny" or "Ask"

📱 Mobile (iOS/Android):

  1. iOS: Settings → Safari → Camera → Ask or Deny
  2. Android Chrome: Settings → Site settings → Camera → Find site and block
  3. Or tap the 🔒 padlock/ⓘ icon in the browser address bar
