Our Mission

CyberSmrt bridges the cybersecurity equity gap by delivering accessible, high-quality education and affordable cybersecurity services to nonprofits, schools, and small businesses. We envision a future where every community, regardless of size or resources, has the knowledge and protection to thrive in an increasingly digital world.

Our Partners

Building strategic relationships to amplify impact and ensure industry alignment

NEAAAT is an innovative STEM-focused charter school partnering with CyberSmrt to bring cutting-edge cybersecurity education to K-12 students.

Tenable is a global leader in exposure management, providing enterprise-grade vulnerability assessment and security tools to help organizations understand and reduce cyber risk.

CompTIA is the world's leading tech association, offering vendor-neutral certifications that validate essential IT and cybersecurity skills for professionals worldwide.

CloudRange delivers hands-on cyber range training platforms that simulate real-world scenarios, enabling teams to practice incident response and security operations in safe, controlled environments.

Cyturity helps organizations move from spreadsheets to automated governance, risk, and compliance. We build systems that keep controls, evidence, and testing running continuously instead of through manual audit cycles. Our approach simplifies compliance across frameworks like SOC 2, HIPAA, and ISO 27001 so teams stay ready every day, not just at audit time.
